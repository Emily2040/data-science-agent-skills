# Evidence Map For Small Data Power Planner

Skill-specific pain point: Small datasets are forced through high-capacity methods, producing unstable conclusions and overconfident narratives.

Skill-specific hypothesis: Power, uncertainty, resampling, priors, and simpler baselines must be planned before modeling scarce data.

## Skill-Specific Evidence Themes

- Small-sample and rare-outcome practice: uncertainty intervals, resampling, regularization, Bayesian priors, and data-collection gates.
- Model complexity is limited by signal, sample size, and decision risk.

## Non-Duplication Position

Existing public tools often cover individual operations such as profiling, validation, tracking, orchestration, visualization, or monitoring. This skill is not a wrapper for one tool. It packages the judgment workflow around tool selection, evidence review, risk classification, and audit-ready reporting.
